## TRPO

- Meaning: TRPO, or trust region policy optimization, is a policy-optimization line that constrains policy updates so the policy does not move too far in one step.
- Why it matters: Together with PPO, it makes the policy-stability bottleneck visible in policy-based reinforcement learning.
- Related concepts: `PPO`, `policy gradient`, `policy-based reinforcement learning`
