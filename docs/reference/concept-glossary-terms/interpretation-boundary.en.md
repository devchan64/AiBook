### interpretation boundary

- Meaning: An interpretation boundary marks how far a data result or comparison can be stated, and where the explanation must stop until more evidence is available.
- Why it matters: A comparison table can show a change signal without proving a cause, diagnosis, or future outcome. Interpretation boundaries keep conservative outputs such as `needs review` separate from stronger claims such as `confirmed anomaly`.
- Related concepts: `interpretation`, `comparison report`, `output structure`, `review queue`
