## max_depth

- Meaning: `max_depth` is a hyperparameter that limits how many levels a decision tree may grow from the root to a leaf. A smaller value keeps the tree closer to broad patterns, while a larger value allows more detailed questions.
- Why it matters: Deeper trees can fit training data more tightly, but they can also memorize small exceptions and overfit. `max_depth` is one of the simplest controls for stopping a tree before it becomes too complex.
- Related concepts: `decision tree`, `leaf`, `overfitting`, `hyperparameter`
