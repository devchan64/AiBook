## REINFORCE

- Meaning: REINFORCE is an early policy-gradient algorithm that uses actions and rewards from an episode to raise the probability of choices that contributed to better outcomes.
- Why it matters: It is a direct introductory example of policy-based reinforcement learning. It helps readers see policy gradient as adjusting action probabilities from reward outcomes.
- Related concepts: `policy gradient`, `policy-based reinforcement learning`, `episode`
