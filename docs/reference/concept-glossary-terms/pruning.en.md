## pruning

- Meaning: Pruning is the process of simplifying an already grown decision tree by removing branches that are too detailed or have weak effect. It can be read as choosing again which branches should remain after the tree has grown.
- Why it matters: Pruning may lower the training score a little, but it can leave a structure that is more stable on new data. It helps readers ask which branches generalize, rather than which branches only improve fit on the training set.
- Related concepts: `decision tree`, `ccp_alpha`, `overfitting`, `generalization`
