## Extra Trees

- Meaning: Extra Trees, or Extremely Randomized Trees, is a tree ensemble that averages predictions from many decision trees. It is close to random forest, but it injects more randomness by drawing split thresholds randomly.
- Why it matters: It is a nearby comparison candidate next to random forest. Under the default setting, `bootstrap=False`, so OOB does not automatically follow.
- Related concepts: `random forest`, `best split`, `random threshold`, `bootstrap`, `oob_score`
