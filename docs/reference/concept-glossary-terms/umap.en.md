## UMAP

- Meaning: UMAP is a dimensionality-reduction visualization method that tries to preserve local-neighbor graph structure from the original space in a lower-dimensional layout.
- Why it matters: UMAP often shows local structure while keeping the plot relatively readable, but the reduced layout is still not a complete copy of the original data.
- Related concepts: `dimensionality reduction`, `graph`, `nearest neighbor`
