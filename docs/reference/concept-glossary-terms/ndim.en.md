## ndim

- Meaning: `ndim` is the number of dimensions, or axes, in an array.
- Why it matters: It helps readers check array structure before reading shape operations, broadcasting, or axis-based computation.
- Related concepts: `shape`, `array`, `dimension`
- Core Section: `P2-11.1`
- Appears in: `P2-11.2`, `P2-11.3`
