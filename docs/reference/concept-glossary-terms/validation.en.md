## validation

- Meaning: Validation is an intermediate evaluation data split or procedure used to compare settings, preprocessing choices, or model options during development.
- Why it matters: Validation keeps model selection separate from final testing. It helps readers distinguish the place where choices are compared from the place where final performance is reported.
- Related concepts: `test`, `data leakage`, `generalization`, `evaluation`
