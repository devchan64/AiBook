## spectral clustering

- Meaning: Spectral clustering represents neighborhood relations as a graph and then forms groups by reading the graph's connectivity structure.
- Why it matters: It can help when curved or irregular structures are hard to read with centers or raw coordinate distance alone. It is still sensitive to how the similarity graph is built, so it should be treated as a modeling choice, not an automatic answer.
- Related concepts: `clustering`, `graph`, `connectivity`, `hierarchical clustering`
