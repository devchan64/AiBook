## perceptron

- Meaning: A perceptron is a simple neural-network decision unit that multiplies several inputs by weights, adds them, adds a bias, and produces an output from the result. It is a basic form of `mix inputs into a score, then decide a response from that score`.
- Why it matters: The perceptron is a starting point for understanding what neural networks compute. Weights, bias, linear combination, and activation all appear here before being stacked into multilayer networks.
- Related concepts: `weight`, `bias`, `linear combination`, `activation function`
