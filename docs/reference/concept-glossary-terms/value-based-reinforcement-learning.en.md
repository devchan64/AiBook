## value-based reinforcement learning

- Meaning: Value-based reinforcement learning is an approach that learns expected long-term reward values for states or state-action pairs, then uses those values to choose better actions. It learns `how good a choice is over time` before or alongside deriving the policy.
- Why it matters: This concept connects algorithms such as Q-learning through their shared focus on value estimates or Q-values. It also separates the value-based view from policy-based reinforcement learning, where the policy itself is adjusted more directly.
- Related concepts: `reinforcement learning`, `state value`, `action value`, `Q-value`, `policy-based reinforcement learning`
