### data quality check

- Meaning: A data quality check is the procedure of checking whether data is usable for the current comparison, learning, or decision purpose by looking for missing values, duplicates, order errors, mixed units, missing records, and similar problems.
- Why it matters: If data-quality problems remain, the model or average calculation may not be the main issue; the input records themselves may not yet be comparable. A data quality check exposes what blocks comparison before calculation starts.
- Related concepts: `format consistency`, `comparability`, `source data`
- Core Section: `P3-2.3`
