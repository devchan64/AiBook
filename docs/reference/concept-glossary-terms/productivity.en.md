## productivity

- Meaning: Productivity is a measure of real efficiency that includes not only generation speed, but also review cost, failure cost, rework cost, and operating cost. It asks how much time and effort are needed to reach a usable final result, not only how quickly the first output appears.
- Why it matters: Fast drafts do not automatically improve the whole work process. This concept separates `the first output was fast` from `the total cost to finish was low`, and evaluates AI tools by speed, quality, reviewability, and reuse together.
- Related concepts: `work automation`, `review`, `evaluation`
