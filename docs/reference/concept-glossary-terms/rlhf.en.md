## RLHF

- Meaning: RLHF, or reinforcement learning from human feedback, is a training flow that interprets human feedback or preference as something reward-like and uses it to adjust a model's output policy.
- Why it matters: It is a representative meeting point between reinforcement-learning reward language and the human-preference problem in LLM alignment.
- Related concepts: `preference optimization`, `reward model`, `alignment`, `policy`
