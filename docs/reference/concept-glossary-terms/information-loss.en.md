## information loss

- Meaning: Information loss is the weakening or disappearance of some detail or relationship when data are summarized, compressed, or transformed.
- Why it matters: A reduced plot can be easier to read without preserving all original structure, so information loss must be checked before treating the plot as evidence.
- Related concepts: `dimensionality reduction`, `representation`, `visualization`
