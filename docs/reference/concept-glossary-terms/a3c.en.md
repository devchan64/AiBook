## A3C

- Meaning: A3C, or asynchronous advantage actor-critic, is a reinforcement-learning family where multiple workers gather experience asynchronously to train an actor-critic structure.
- Why it matters: It often appears with A2C when reading how actor-critic methods were made more practical.
- Related concepts: `A2C`, `actor-critic`, `policy gradient`
