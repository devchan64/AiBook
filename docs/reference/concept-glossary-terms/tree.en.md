## tree

- Meaning: A tree is a structure that organizes items through parent-child hierarchy. It emphasizes which item sits under a higher item and how lower items continue to branch.
- Why it matters: Tables of contents, folders, classification schemes, and decision flows often need a tree-like hierarchy. The concept helps readers distinguish hierarchical branching from ordinary order, table structure, and more general graph structure.
- Related concepts: `graph`, `linear structure`, `non-linear structure`
