## safe reinforcement learning

- Meaning: Safe reinforcement learning is a reinforcement-learning branch that treats safety constraints together with policy improvement in order to reduce dangerous actions, damage, or liability.
- Why it matters: In real applications, exploration can itself create cost and risk, so performance must be read together with failure tolerance and constraints.
- Related concepts: `safe exploration`, `offline reinforcement learning`, `exploration cost`
