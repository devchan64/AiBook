## domain randomization

- Meaning: Domain randomization is a sim-to-real reinforcement strategy that intentionally varies simulator conditions so a policy becomes less brittle to real-world differences.
- Why it matters: Because a simulator cannot perfectly copy reality, the policy should not overfit to one overly clean simulated setting.
- Related concepts: `sim-to-real gap`, `simulation`, `policy`
