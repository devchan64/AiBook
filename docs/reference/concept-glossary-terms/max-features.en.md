## max_features

- Meaning: `max_features` is a hyperparameter that controls how many features, or what fraction of features, each split may consider in a random forest.
- Why it matters: `max_features` affects how similar the trees become. If it is too large, many trees may follow the same strong feature; if it is too small, individual trees may become too weak.
- Related concepts: `random forest`, `feature`, `hyperparameter`, `ensemble`
