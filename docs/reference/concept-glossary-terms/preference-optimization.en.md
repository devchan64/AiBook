## preference optimization

- Meaning: Preference optimization is a training flow that uses human comparisons, ratings, or preference data to adjust model outputs toward more desirable behavior.
- Why it matters: In LLM alignment, preference comparisons are often more natural than direct correct labels, so this term becomes a key handle next to RLHF.
- Related concepts: `RLHF`, `preference data`, `reward model`, `alignment`
