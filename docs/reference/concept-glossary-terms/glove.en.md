## GloVe

- Meaning: GloVe is an embedding method that learns word vectors by reflecting global word-word co-occurrence statistics.
- Why it matters: If word2vec gives the intuition of learning from neighboring-context prediction, GloVe shows that corpus-wide co-occurrence statistics can also shape word representations. It helps readers compare embedding families by the signal used to arrange the representation space.
- Related concepts: `word2vec`, `embedding`, `distributed representation`
