## high-cardinality feature

- Meaning: A high-cardinality feature is a feature with many distinct values, such as customer IDs, order numbers, or raw timestamps.
- Why it matters: In tree-based models, features with many possible values can be easy to split finely in the training data, so their importance can appear larger than their real contribution to generalization.
- Related concepts: `feature importance`, `mean decrease in impurity(MDI)`, `overfitting`
