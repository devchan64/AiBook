## A2C

- Meaning: A2C, or advantage actor-critic, is a reinforcement-learning family that uses an actor for the policy and a critic for value information, guided by an advantage signal.
- Why it matters: It is a beginner handle for reading why actor-critic methods try to combine direct policy learning with value information.
- Related concepts: `actor-critic`, `policy gradient`, `A3C`
