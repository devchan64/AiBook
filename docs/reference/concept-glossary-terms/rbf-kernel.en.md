## RBF kernel, radial basis function kernel

- Meaning: A common kernel that strongly reflects distance and local similarity between points. Nearby points are read as more similar, while similarity drops quickly as points move farther apart.
- Why it matters: When data show circular, clustered, or curved local structure, an RBF kernel becomes a candidate for reading patterns that feel awkward under a linear boundary alone.
- Related concepts: `kernel`, `feature space`, `distance`, `SVM`
