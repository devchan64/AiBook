## eigenvector

- Meaning: An eigenvector is a direction vector whose direction stays the same when a matrix acts on it, even though its length may change.
- Why it matters: In PCA, eigenvectors are read as the directions of new axes that explain the spread of the data.
- Related concepts: `eigenvalue`, `covariance matrix`, `PCA`
