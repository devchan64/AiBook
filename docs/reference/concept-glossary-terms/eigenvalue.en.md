## eigenvalue

- Meaning: An eigenvalue tells how much a matrix stretches or shrinks a special direction vector while keeping that direction the same.
- Why it matters: In PCA, eigenvalues can be read as the amount of variation explained by each new axis from the covariance structure.
- Related concepts: `eigenvector`, `covariance matrix`, `PCA`
