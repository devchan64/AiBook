### representation-space topology

- Meaning: Representation-space topology is the more abstract mathematical viewpoint that asks how points in an AI representation space form structures of nearness, connectedness, and continuity. If position in P2-3.2 means the coordinate-like place of one vector, topology looks more broadly at the structure of the whole space. It is not the same as a single position.
- Why it matters: In AI writing, phrases such as `topology of a representation space` or `data manifold` often refer to the overall structure made by data representations rather than individual vector coordinates. This distinction prevents beginners from collapsing position, distance, and topology into one vague space metaphor. Here, topology is used only as a marker for deeper structure, not as a full topology course.
- Related concepts: `vector space`, `dimension`, `representation`
