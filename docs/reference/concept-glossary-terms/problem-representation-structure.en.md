### problem-representation structure

- Meaning: A problem-representation structure is a redesigned form of stored records that expresses the current question through samples, derived columns, comparison criteria, and output form.
- Why it matters: It lets stored records become a structure for comparison, interpretation, and learning preparation instead of only a preservation table.
- Related concepts: `storage structure`, `dataset candidate`, `data modeling`, `output structure`
- Core Section: `P3-2.1`
- Appears in: `P3-index`, `P3-3.1`, `P3-3.2`, `P3-3.3`, `P3-summary`
