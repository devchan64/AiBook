## hierarchical clustering

- Meaning: Hierarchical clustering is a clustering method that shows how data points or smaller groups become close and merge step by step.
- Why it matters: It lets readers inspect multiple grouping levels instead of fixing one cluster count from the beginning. The result is useful not only for asking how many groups there are, but also for asking what merges first.
- Related concepts: `clustering`, `cluster`, `distance`, `graph`
