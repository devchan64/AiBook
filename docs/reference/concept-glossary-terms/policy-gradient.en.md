## policy gradient

- Meaning: Policy gradient is a family of reinforcement-learning methods that directly adjusts policy parameters in a direction expected to increase return. The basic idea is to make actions that led to better rewards more likely and actions that led to worse rewards less likely.
- Why it matters: It shows that a policy can be treated as an adjustable function, not only as a fixed action rule. This concept connects early policy-gradient algorithms, actor-critic, and later policy-optimization methods.
- Related concepts: `policy-based reinforcement learning`, `actor-critic`, `expected return`, `policy`
