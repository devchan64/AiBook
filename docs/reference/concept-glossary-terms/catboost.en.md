## CatBoost

- Meaning: CatBoost is a gradient-boosting library that emphasizes categorical-feature handling and ordered boosting. It is often discussed through categorical-data processing and mitigation of target leakage.
- Why it matters: CatBoost shows that choosing a boosting implementation can depend on how safely categorical data are handled. It becomes especially relevant when there are many categorical columns or when target-encoding leakage is a concern.
- Related concepts: `gradient boosting`, `categorical feature`, `leakage`
