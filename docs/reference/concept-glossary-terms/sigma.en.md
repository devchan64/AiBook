## sigma

- Meaning: Sigma is the summation symbol that says to repeat additions of the same form. It states which index changes, what term is added, and over what range the additions run.
- Why it matters: Means, total losses, and probability calculations often collect many terms by one rule. Reading sigma helps readers expand a compact formula into repeated calculation or array calculation.
- Related concepts: `index`, `mean`, `loss`
