### role-aware reading across heterogeneous scales

- Meaning: A way of reading numerical columns with different units and ranges by their role and same-column baseline-relative change, rather than by comparing raw magnitudes directly. If duration, pressure, variability, and rate of change measure different axes, the larger number is not automatically the more important one.
- Why it matters: Columns in the same feature table can still have different units, ranges, and widths of variation. This view helps readers avoid treating large numbers as more important features, and it prepares them to understand why scale-sensitive models such as distance-based models can behave differently after scaling.
- Related concepts: `feature`, `baseline`, `k-NN`, `preprocessing`
