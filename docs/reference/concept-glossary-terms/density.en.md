## density

- Meaning: Density describes how tightly data points are packed in a region of the feature space.
- Why it matters: Not every cluster is round or evenly sized. Density helps explain grouping methods that find crowded regions and leave sparse points aside, especially in DBSCAN-style clustering.
- Related concepts: `clustering`, `distance`, `similarity`, `DBSCAN`
