## polynomial kernel

- Meaning: A kernel that makes polynomial interactions such as squares, products, and higher-degree feature combinations easier to read.
- Why it matters: When combinations among features seem more important than any single feature, a polynomial kernel helps emphasize those interactions inside the representation space.
- Related concepts: `kernel`, `feature space`, `interaction feature`, `SVM`
