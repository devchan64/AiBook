## offline reinforcement learning

- Meaning: Offline reinforcement learning is a reinforcement-learning branch that tries to learn a policy mostly from already collected data rather than from many new exploratory trials.
- Why it matters: When direct trial is expensive or risky, the first question becomes what can be evaluated and improved inside existing data.
- Related concepts: `safe reinforcement learning`, `exploration cost`, `policy`
