## scatter plot

- Meaning: A scatter plot draws each sample as a point to show the relationship and spread between two variables.
- Why it matters: It helps readers quickly inspect whether two values move together, whether points form groups, and whether outliers or nonlinear patterns may be present.
- Related concepts: `plot`, `distribution`, `sample`
