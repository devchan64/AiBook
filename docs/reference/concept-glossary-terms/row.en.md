### row

- Meaning: A row is the horizontal line in a table that represents one case, observation, or sample unit. In real data, one row may already be one training sample, or several rows may need to be grouped into one event.
- Why it matters: When reading a DataFrame, the first question is whether one row is the final sample unit or only part of a larger action or case. If row interpretation is unstable, aggregation, labeling, and dataset construction become unstable as well.
- Related concepts: `column`, `DataFrame`, `dataset`
