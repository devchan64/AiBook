## covariance matrix

- Meaning: A covariance matrix records how multiple features vary on their own and how pairs of features move together.
- Why it matters: PCA uses this structure to find directions where the data spread strongly; its eigenvectors become candidate component directions, and its eigenvalues indicate variation along those directions.
- Related concepts: `covariance`, `variance`, `PCA`
