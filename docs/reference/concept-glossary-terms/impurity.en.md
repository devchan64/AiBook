## impurity

- Meaning: In a decision-tree node, impurity describes how mixed the classes are. Classification trees can compare before-and-after mixing with criteria such as gini, entropy, or log loss.
- Why it matters: A good split often turns one mixed node into less mixed nodes. Impurity helps the reader see decision-tree training as a comparison of how much each question organizes labels, not as a purely intuitive choice of rules.
- Related concepts: `decision tree`, `split`, `leaf`, `gini`
