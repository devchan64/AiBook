## policy gradient theorem

- Meaning: The policy gradient theorem connects changes in policy parameters to the direction in which expected reward increases.
- Why it matters: It explains why the intuition of directly adjusting a policy appears as formulas that combine reward with gradients of policy probabilities.
- Related concepts: `policy gradient`, `likelihood ratio trick`, `expected reward`, `REINFORCE`
