## DQN

- Meaning: DQN, or deep Q-network, is a value-based reinforcement-learning family that represents Q-values with a function approximator such as a neural network instead of a table.
- Why it matters: It shows how the value-based intuition can be extended when the state space becomes too large for a Q-table.
- Related concepts: `Q-learning`, `Q-value`, `value-based reinforcement learning`, `function approximation`
