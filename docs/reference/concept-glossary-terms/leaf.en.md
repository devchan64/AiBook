## leaf

- Meaning: The endpoint of a decision tree where no more questions are asked and a final prediction is produced. In classification it can output a class or class proportions; in regression it can output a representative number such as an average.
- Why it matters: A leaf shows which cases have gathered after several splits and what prediction they receive. Even when scores look similar, a leaf that still mixes many classes needs careful interpretation.
- Related concepts: `decision tree`, `split`, `tree`, `impurity`
