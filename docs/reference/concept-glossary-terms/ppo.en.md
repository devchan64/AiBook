## PPO

- Meaning: PPO, or proximal policy optimization, is a policy-based reinforcement-learning family that tries to keep direct policy updates from changing too abruptly.
- Why it matters: It is a representative handle for reading policy-stabilization problems before treating later reinforcement-learning names as generic performance upgrades.
- Related concepts: `policy-based reinforcement learning`, `policy gradient`, `actor-critic`, `TRPO`
