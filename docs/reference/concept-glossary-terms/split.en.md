## split

- Meaning: In a decision tree, a split is the act of dividing data into two or more branches according to a question or criterion. For numeric features, it often takes the form of a condition such as `x <= threshold`.
- Why it matters: A decision tree builds its prediction structure by repeatedly choosing useful splits. Understanding splits helps the reader see the tree not as a list of rules written by a person, but as a model that chooses questions that make the current node less mixed.
- Related concepts: `decision tree`, `tree`, `impurity`, `threshold`
