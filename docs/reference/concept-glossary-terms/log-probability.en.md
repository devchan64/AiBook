## log-probability

- Meaning: Log-probability is the logarithm of the probability of an event or chosen action.
- Why it matters: In policy-based reinforcement learning, forms such as `log pi(a|s)` are often used to connect the probability of a selected action to the update calculation.
- Related concepts: `likelihood ratio trick`, `policy gradient`, `REINFORCE`
