### review queue

- Meaning: A review queue is an operational output that orders candidate cases by what a person should inspect first.
- Why it matters: Not every detected change can become a prediction problem or confirmed label immediately. A review queue creates an intermediate priority structure between automated comparison results and human review work.
- Related concepts: `comparison report`, `output structure`, `target candidate`
