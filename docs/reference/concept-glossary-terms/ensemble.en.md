## ensemble

- Meaning: An ensemble combines predictions from multiple models into one final judgment. It may use averaging, voting, or sequential correction to obtain a result that is more stable or stronger than one model alone.
- Why it matters: Ensemble methods include random forests, bagging, and gradient boosting, but these methods combine models in different ways. The key question is not only `how many models are used`, but `how their predictions are combined`.
- Related concepts: `random forest`, `gradient boosting`, `averaging`, `voting`
