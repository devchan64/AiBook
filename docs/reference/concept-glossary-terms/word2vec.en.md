## word2vec

- Meaning: word2vec is a family of methods for efficiently learning word embeddings from the contexts in which words appear together. Instead of writing dictionary meanings by hand, it learns word vectors from neighboring-word patterns in a corpus.
- Why it matters: word2vec gave many learners the first practical intuition that word meaning can be learned as a vector representation from usage context. It does not explain every contextual representation used by modern LLMs, but it is an important bridge toward the idea that words can be placed in vector space and similar words can become close.
- Related concepts: `embedding`, `distributed representation`, `corpus`
