## personal learning

- Meaning: Personal learning is the process of recovering understanding by relearning forgotten concepts, organizing questions, and checking evidence. It is closer to identifying what is missing and filling those gaps again than to simple memorization.
- Why it matters: Personal learning shows a practical use of AI as a relearning aid rather than an answer machine. It shifts attention from `what answer should I memorize?` to `what question path helps rebuild understanding?` It also separates using AI to replace an answer from using AI to recover concepts and organize explanations.
- Related concepts: `documentation`, `factual claim`, `evidence review`
