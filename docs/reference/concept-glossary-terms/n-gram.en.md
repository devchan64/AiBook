## n-gram

- Meaning: An n-gram is a contiguous group of `n` words, characters, or tokens used as a short local context unit.
- Why it matters: It shows how earlier language models counted short nearby fragments before modern models handled richer and longer context.
- Related concepts: `language model`, `statistical language model`, `sparsity`
- Core Section: `P1-11.1`
- Appears in: `P1-9.3`, `P6-19.1`
