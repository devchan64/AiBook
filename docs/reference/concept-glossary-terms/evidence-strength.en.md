### evidence strength

- Meaning: Evidence strength is how solid the basis is for stating a difference or signal with a certain level of confidence. In this section, it refers to reading observation conditions such as sample size, repeatability, and baseline conditions together because they change the interpretation strength of the same `diff`.
- Why it matters: If we look only at the difference value, coincidence from a small sample can be overread as a strong warning. Evidence strength lets the same difference be stated at different sentence strengths, such as record only, review candidate, or stronger warning.
- Related concepts: `evidence`, `comparison table`, `review queue`, `evaluation`
