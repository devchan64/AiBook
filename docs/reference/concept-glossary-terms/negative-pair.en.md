## negative pair

- Meaning: A negative pair is a pair of inputs given as examples that should become farther apart in representation learning.
- Why it matters: If negative pairs are chosen incorrectly, different problem scenes can mix into the same candidate group and retrieval quality can shake. The term marks what the model is being asked to learn as different.
- Related concepts: `contrastive learning`, `positive pair`, `embedding`
