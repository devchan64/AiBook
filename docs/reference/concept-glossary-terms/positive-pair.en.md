## positive pair

- Meaning: A positive pair is a pair of inputs given as examples that should become closer in representation learning.
- Why it matters: If positive pairs are chosen incorrectly, same-intent expressions can be placed far apart and retrieval candidates can scatter. The term marks what the model is being asked to learn as similar.
- Related concepts: `contrastive learning`, `negative pair`, `embedding`
