## exploration cost

- Meaning: Exploration cost is the loss that can be paid when a reinforcement-learning reinforcement learning agent tries new actions. It can include time loss, equipment damage, user churn, accidents, or legal responsibility.
- Why it matters: Exploration gathers information, but in real systems it can also create failure cost. This concept keeps average reward from being read without safe-exploration and stopping criteria.
- Related concepts: `exploration`, `safe exploration`, `error cost`, `deployment`
