## off-policy

- Meaning: Off-policy learning learns values for a policy that is not necessarily the same as the policy currently producing behavior. For example, it can update from the best-looking next action rather than only from the action actually taken next.
- Why it matters: It explains how learning can aim toward a target or optimal policy even while the actual behavior includes exploration. This is the key contrast with on-policy learning.
- Related concepts: `on-policy`, `policy`, `exploration`, `value-based reinforcement learning`
