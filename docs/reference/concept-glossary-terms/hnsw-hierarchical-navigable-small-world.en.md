## HNSW, hierarchical navigable small world

- Meaning: HNSW is a graph-based vector search index method that finds approximate nearest neighbors by navigating links among nearby vectors.
- Why it matters: It shows that large-scale vector search is not only comparing vectors, but also building paths through an index so nearby candidates can be reached quickly. Readers do not need to implement HNSW here, but the term explains why graph-based ANN appears often in vector databases.
- Related concepts: `graph-based search`, `ANN, approximate nearest neighbor`, `search index`, `vector database`
