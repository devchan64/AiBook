### alert

- Meaning: The lightest problem stage, where a difference from a baseline or usual state is shown so a person can look first. It does not yet mean a cause label or confirmed judgment. It is closer to `a change is visible, so check it`.
- Why it matters: In real data, a change signal alone is often not enough to create a prediction problem. Keeping alert as a separate stage separates what comparison structure and difference values can support from the stronger evidence needed for review candidates or label prediction.
- Related concepts: `baseline`, `comparison report`, `review candidate`, `evidence strength`
