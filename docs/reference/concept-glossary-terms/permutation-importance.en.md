## permutation importance

- Meaning: Permutation importance estimates feature importance by randomly shuffling one feature and measuring how much model performance drops.
- Why it matters: While MDI summarizes internal split usage, permutation importance checks how much prediction performance depends on a feature when that feature is broken.
- Related concepts: `feature importance`, `mean decrease in impurity(MDI)`, `correlated features`
