## DBSCAN

- Meaning: DBSCAN is a density-based clustering algorithm that forms clusters from dense neighborhoods and can leave low-density points as noise.
- Why it matters: Unlike methods that force every point into a fixed number of clusters, DBSCAN can keep outlier-like points outside the discovered groups. It helps readers distinguish center-based grouping from density-based structure discovery.
- Related concepts: `clustering`, `density`, `noise`, `outlier`
