## formula

- Meaning: A formula is a compact expression of a calculation relationship or procedure using symbols and numbers. It can summarize how values are added, compared, divided, transformed, or optimized.
- Why it matters: AI documents often compress long calculation procedures into one formula. Reading formulas helps readers expand a compact expression back into inputs, operations, outputs, and code.
