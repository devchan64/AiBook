## trustworthiness

- Meaning: Trustworthiness is a visualization quality metric that checks whether neighbors that look close in a low-dimensional plot were also close in the original high-dimensional space.
- Why it matters: It helps prevent overtrusting 2D proximity by asking how many false close-neighbor relations the projection created.
- Related concepts: `dimensionality reduction`, `nearest neighbor`, `visualization`
