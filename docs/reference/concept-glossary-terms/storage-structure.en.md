### storage structure

- Meaning: A storage structure is a data structure designed to preserve records and make them traceable.
- Why it matters: Storage structures are good at preserving evidence, but they do not automatically define the sample unit or comparison columns needed for a learnable dataset candidate.
- Related concepts: `source data`, `dataset candidate`, `problem-representation structure`, `row`
- Core Section: `P3-2.1`
- Appears in: `P3-index`, `P3-3.2`, `P3-summary`
