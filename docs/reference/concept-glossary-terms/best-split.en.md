## best split

- Meaning: A best split is the split criterion chosen at a decision-tree node because it reduces impurity best among the considered candidates.
- Why it matters: It helps distinguish random forest from Extra Trees: random forest searches for better splits among candidates, while Extra Trees randomizes split thresholds more strongly.
- Related concepts: `decision tree`, `impurity`, `random threshold`, `Extra Trees`
