## method

- Meaning: A method is a function-like behavior attached to an object, usually called as `value.method()`.
- Why it matters: It helps readers understand object-oriented calls such as `df.head()`, `model.fit()`, and `text.lower()`.
- Related concepts: `function`, `value`, `type`
- Core Section: `P2-8.5`
- Appears in: `P2-8.6`, `P2-12.2`
