## likelihood ratio trick

- Meaning: The likelihood ratio trick rewrites difficult differentiation of a probability distribution into a log-probability gradient form that is easier to handle inside an expectation.
- Why it matters: It explains why forms such as `log pi(a|s)` repeatedly appear in REINFORCE and policy-gradient formulas.
- Related concepts: `log-probability`, `policy gradient theorem`, `REINFORCE`
