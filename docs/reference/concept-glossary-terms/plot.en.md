## plot

- Meaning: A plot is a visual representation that turns numeric or tabular data into lines, points, bars, regions, or other visible shapes. It is not decoration; it is a way to read relationships, change, and distribution by sight.
- Why it matters: Tables are strong at exact values, while plots are strong at revealing patterns that are hard to notice in rows of numbers. Understanding plots helps readers ask what the visual form shows and what it does not prove.
- Related concepts: `visualization`, `axis`, `comparison plot`
