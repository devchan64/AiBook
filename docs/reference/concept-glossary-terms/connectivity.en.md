## connectivity

- Meaning: Connectivity describes how data points are linked through neighborhood or graph relationships.
- Why it matters: Raw coordinate distance can cut curved or irregular structures in awkward ways. Connectivity helps readers understand graph-based clustering methods, especially spectral clustering, where the structure of links matters before the final grouping.
- Related concepts: `graph`, `spectral clustering`, `similarity`
