## safe exploration

- Meaning: Safe exploration is the problem of letting a reinforcement-learning agent try new actions while preventing unacceptable failures such as accidents, damage, user harm, or legal risk.
- Why it matters: In real environments, exploration itself can create cost and risk. This concept corrects the simple intuition that more trials automatically mean better learning.
- Related concepts: `exploration`, `exploration cost`, `reward design`, `safe RL`
