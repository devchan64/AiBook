### skewness

- Meaning: Skewness is the degree to which values are not spread symmetrically around the center, with one tail stretching farther than the other. In a skewed distribution, the center and the direction of the long tail both matter.
- Why it matters: When skewness is large, the mean may not represent where most cases actually sit. This concept helps readers check the mean together with distribution shape, quantiles, and outliers instead of treating one center value as the whole distribution.
- Related concepts: `mean`, `distribution`, `outlier`
