## reconstruction error

- Meaning: Reconstruction error is the difference between original values and values restored from a reduced or compressed representation.
- Why it matters: It gives a concrete signal of what was lost when dimensionality reduction preserved a large flow but discarded some detail.
- Related concepts: `dimensionality reduction`, `PCA`, `information loss`

- Appears in: `P4-18.1`
