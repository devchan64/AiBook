## hierarchy

- Meaning: A hierarchy is a structure with upper and lower levels, moving from broader categories to narrower ones. Tables of contents, folders, and classification systems are common examples.
- Why it matters: Hierarchy explains why a tree is different from a flat list or a general graph. It gives readers a way to ask which level they are reading and how parent-child structure organizes information.
- Related concepts: `tree`, `non-linear structure`, `data structure`
