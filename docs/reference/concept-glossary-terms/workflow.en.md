## workflow

- Meaning: A workflow is the operating pattern that connects steps such as writing, review, and deployment in a defined order with defined criteria. It describes how steps relate to each other, not just a list of commands or tools.
- Why it matters: Without a workflow, draft judgments and public-release standards can blur together. The concept helps readers separate drafting, checking, and deployment as different roles in one document process.
- Related concepts: `branch`, `deployment`, `document reproducibility`
