## expected reward

- Meaning: Expected reward is the reward a policy is expected to receive on average over possible actions, outcomes, or trajectories.
- Why it matters: Policy gradient moves policy parameters in the direction that increases expected reward, so the formula must be read through an expectation rather than one observed reward alone.
- Related concepts: `reward`, `policy gradient theorem`, `return`
