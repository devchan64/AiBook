## support vector

- Meaning: A data case closest to the classification boundary in SVM, effectively helping hold the boundary in place. Intuitively, these are the tightest points that would be affected first if the boundary moved.
- Why it matters: Not every training case determines the boundary equally. Support vectors show which cases narrow the margin and influence boundary stability, making near-boundary review and error analysis more concrete.
- Related concepts: `SVM`, `margin`, `decision boundary`
