## t-SNE

- Meaning: t-SNE is a dimensionality-reduction visualization method that arranges points so nearby-neighbor relations from the original high-dimensional space appear similarly in low dimensions.
- Why it matters: t-SNE can make local groups look clear, but distances between groups and the overall layout require caution.
- Related concepts: `dimensionality reduction`, `nearest neighbor`, `trustworthiness`
