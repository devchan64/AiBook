# AiBook (English Edition)

This English edition follows the same curriculum structure, `Section ID` system, and shared section release notes as the Korean edition.

The book is designed for readers who are rebuilding their AI fundamentals, reconnecting older introductory knowledge to modern machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, generative AI, service architecture, and project practice.

Part 1 is the current entry point of the English edition. It begins by restoring the overall map before moving into detailed implementation and later technical Parts.
