# P2-5.5 Supplemental Learning: How to First Read Standard Deviation, Correlation, and Confidence Intervals

> Section ID: `P2-5.5`
> Version: `v2026.07.09`

After probability, mean, variance, sample, estimation, and error, statistics writing quickly introduces more terms. This supplemental Section is not here to master all formulas. It is here to separate what each term is trying to read.

## Scope of This Supplement

This Section gives an entry-level reading of standard deviation, covariance, correlation coefficient, standard error, confidence interval, and hypothesis testing.

## One Reason This Supplement Exists

These terms often look like one crowded block of “more statistics,” but they answer different kinds of questions.

## Keep This Separation

- standard deviation: how spread out values are
- correlation: how two variables move together
- standard error: how uncertain an estimate is
- confidence interval: plausible range around an estimate
- hypothesis testing: procedure for checking whether an observed pattern is strong enough to question a baseline assumption

## Perspective to Keep

- These are not identical extensions of the mean.
- They become easier once each term is tied to a different question.
- This Section is for role recovery, not full inferential statistics mastery.

## Short Check

- Can you explain how estimate-uncertainty questions differ from raw-data-spread questions?
