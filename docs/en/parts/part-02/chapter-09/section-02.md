# P2-9.2 The Intuition of Arrays, Tables, Trees, and Graphs

> Section ID: `P2-9.2`
> Version: `v2026.07.09`

P2-9.1 treated data structures as a question of organizing shape. This Section compares four broad shapes that appear often in AI practice: array, table, tree, and graph.

## Scope of This Section

This Section focuses on reading questions rather than full implementations.

## Central Question

Why does the natural structure change depending on whether we are looking at position, rows and columns, hierarchy, or relationships?

## Perspective to Keep

- Arrays emphasize position and axis.
- Tables emphasize rows, columns, and case comparison.
- Trees emphasize hierarchy.
- Graphs emphasize relationships.

## Short Check

- Can you explain why the same data may be reorganized differently when the question changes?
- Can you distinguish the four structures by the type of question they make easier?
- Can you explain why “which structure?” is often really “which question first?”?
