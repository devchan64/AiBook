P2-13.1 introduces plots as tools for seeing shape, change, spread, and comparison more quickly than a table alone.

## Scope of This Section

This Section introduces the role of plots, the distinction between `Figure` and `Axes`, and the idea that a chart begins with a question.

## Central Question

What can a plot reveal that a table of exact values does not reveal as quickly?

## Perspective to Keep

- Tables are strong at exact values; plots are strong at visible shape.
- A chart should begin with a reading question, not with a memorized chart name.
- `Figure` is the whole canvas, and `Axes` is the actual coordinate area where data appears.
- A plot can suggest a pattern without proving its cause.
