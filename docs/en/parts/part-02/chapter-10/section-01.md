Chapter 7 separated execution environments. Chapters 8 and 9 recovered Python syntax and data structures. Now the focus shifts to a format that keeps code, explanation, and output together: the notebook.

## Scope of This Section

This Section introduces `notebook`, `code cell`, `markdown cell`, and `output`.

## Central Question

Why is a notebook especially useful when learning through small experiments, calculations, tables, and plots?

## Perspective to Keep

- A notebook is a computational document.
- Code cells, markdown cells, and outputs play different roles.
- Notebooks are strong for learning and exploration, but they do not replace scripts in every situation.
